# Welcome

You are being invited to participate in a study titled *"Usefulness of Automatically Generated Genomics Visualizations"*. This study is
conducted by researchers at Harvard Medical School.

The purpose of this study is to find out how useful genomics visualizations produced
automatically by a language model are for researchers, and whether automated quality scores
agree with the judgement of people who work with this kind of data.

Please open this experiment in **Chrome or Firefox on a desktop or laptop**. The visualizations
need hardware-accelerated graphics and a wide screen, and they will not work on a phone or
tablet. **You cannot navigate back to previous pages.**

Please respond to this study only if you:

* are a fluent speaker of English
* are of legal age (18 years in most countries)
* work with genomic or biomedical data, or read visualizations of it as part of your work

This experiment will take around 65 minutes.

We thank you for your interest in participating in this study. Please click the "Next" button
below to proceed to the informed consent.
